# Behavioral cases

These are acceptance scenarios for evaluating the skill, not claims of completed live runs. For a forward test, give the runner the raw scenario and skill, then compare the observed behavior with the criteria. Use synthetic inputs clearly labeled as such; do not represent test reviews or product claims as real research.

## Release review scope

Check meaningful invariants: evidence applicability, source availability honesty, six secondary images plus exactly five A+ image assets, per-image prompts, visual-source-to-design links, internal versus visible text, prompt independence, no unsolicited generation, and readiness separation. Structural YAML validation alone is insufficient. Partial browser recovery checks do not establish full 3–6-competitor research or a complete production run.

For scene/A+ revisions also check candidate compatibility and exclusions, user-overridable regional defaults, task diversity, fixed-decoration identity, five-image narrative and module accounting, palette/type roles, and physically coherent light/shadow decisions. For 1.4.0 check scale/projection versus frame consistency, bounded outside inspiration search, prop salience, complete product protection from typography effects, expressive-type limits and canvas/slot/export separation. For 1.5.0 check whole-set coverage, post-research popup choices, delegation versus silence, portable library provenance, blueprint fidelity and exact-text non-PDF collections. These 62 scenarios are a maintained acceptance set; adding a row or reviewing its rules does not mean an independent runner has passed it.
