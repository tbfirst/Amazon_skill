# Amazon Plant Image Strategy

Help the seller discover what buyers need to understand before purchasing, then turn supported product facts and market evidence into a coherent image campaign. Deliver strategy and prompts; the user copies the prompts and uploads the mapped references into Lovart. Do not generate images, operate Lovart, publish listings, or assume paid research access as part of this skill.

## Defaults and scope

- Marketplace: Amazon US. Conversation and strategy: Chinese. Customer-facing image copy and Lovart prompts: English. Explicit project choices override these defaults.
- Lifestyle setting: recognizable US/European residential architecture and household styling, localized to the marketplace; default US projects to plausible American homes. Describe the architecture positively and avoid unrequested Asian/African themed decor. This is a setting preference, not a restriction on people or ethnicities. Explicit regional, cultural or product-use directions override it.
- Ask the user to state what the product is, upload original product materials (measurements, real photos, white-background and multi-angle details as available), and supply **3–6 unique competitor ASINs or links**. Do not require finished secondary or A+ images as input. Inspect existing uploads before requesting missing essentials.
- Deliver exactly **11 image plans and prompts: DIM01, DETAIL01, SCENE01–SCENE04, APLUS01–APLUS05**. The six listing images are secondary images; no new MAIN is included. Five A+ images means five image assets, not five arbitrarily expanding modules. Explicit later changes override this default.
- Default production canvases: **1600×1600 px for the six secondary images; 1464×600 px for the five desktop A+ images**. These are the user's reusable production defaults, not a claim that every Amazon module accepts this size. Use these concrete canvases without re-asking; keep account-slot/upload review separate. Explicit later project specifications override them.
- Research competitors in isolated browser tabs/sessions using [browser research](references/browser-research.md). When the optional `amazon-variant-gallery-scraper` skill is installed, discover its actual path and read [competitor collection](references/competitor-collection.md) first. Read its current concurrency/locking contract; gallery and A+ capture supports module provenance where available, while reviews require separate research. A direct-fetch failure begins recovery. After bounded recovery, use bundled historical sets for layout dialogue while preserving genuine research gaps; the scraper and an author's local disk are not prerequisites for using this skill.
- Exhaust the applicable browser and public-search recovery routes before requesting the smallest missing export. Continue unaffected work and allow a clearly incomplete draft. Never turn missing data into zero demand, positive findings, or invented metrics.
- One project concerns one saleable SKU/variant unless a wider scope is requested. Distinguish live, artificial, dried, and preserved products; do not silently treat them as interchangeable.
- This skill is self-contained. The separate `lovart-plant-product-images` skill can execute individual image tasks when separately requested; it is not a dependency.

## Start with a conversation

Read [discovery](references/discovery.md). Default to frequent, iterative popup dialogue to understand the user's intent; do not minimize the number of rounds at the expense of clarity. Inspect supplied materials, then ask about ambiguous image roles, SKU identity, measurement targets and endpoints as soon as they affect a decision. Use the current mode's available text-input popup tool, with two or three concrete options when useful and free-text questions for unknown facts; never recommend a guessed measurement as a fact. After competitor visual research, discuss concrete layouts for every asset through [set layout dialogue](references/set-layout-dialogue.md), including follow-up choices about framing, detail windows, text, dimensions, backgrounds and lighting. Show reference images/links in chat before the popup when useful. If no popup tool is available, ask in chat. Wait for unresolved answers; do not submit the preselected option on the user's behalf. Request uploads in chat, not a text-only popup.

Persist the developing brief in `discovery.md`: confirmed facts and decisions, inferred ideas, unanswered questions, contradictions, and current stage. On resume, read it and continue from the open decision. New evidence can revise strategy; record affected assets instead of restarting the interview.

Collect and research while maintaining a live dialogue. Use short popup batches as many times as needed: clarify materials and facts, compare layouts, refine each asset, then draft the confirmed direction. Each answer should inform the next useful question. Skip choices already answered or explicitly delegated; an overall style choice does not delegate all individual layouts. A user request for immediate output permits reasoned design assumptions, never invented product facts. Do not re-ask the fixed output count or canvases.

## Establish evidence before claims

Read [research and evidence](references/research-evidence.md) and [browser research](references/browser-research.md). Inventory photos by filename and role, inspect relevant images, and construct separate source, product, competitor-visual, review, keyword, intent, and risk records. For every ASIN, inspect its gallery, A+ visuals, customer photos and review text as accessible; record each section's actual coverage. Every customer-facing factual claim and factual visual implication must trace to evidence covering this SKU and this claim. Evidence IDs are internal metadata, not image text.

Execute research in passes: verify every competitor first, inventory gallery/A+ sections second, inspect accessible static images and sample available review text third, then compare and transfer findings. Read embedded product-detail-page reviews before trying the full review list; track these routes separately and preserve readable originals when the list requires sign-in or fails. The review budget is a ceiling, not a minimum. Preserve each competitor's ordered gallery and A+ narrative, common design rules and layout changes across the set; a thumbnail grid is an entry point, not a substitute for inspecting full images. Save each ASIN checkpoint after a section, so one slow page does not block the entire batch. Follow the coverage and recovery stopping criteria in browser research. Use [research records](references/research-records.md) and the bundled offline audit helper when Python is available; it normalizes inputs and audits supplied evidence records, but does not browse or prove observations itself.

Keep three distinct concepts: a source's reliability, what it actually proves, and how confidently a strategic inference follows. Competitor reviews prove sampled buyer concerns, not our product's performance. SQP and tool estimates do not automatically prove a competitor's organic visits. A risk score never authorizes an unsupported or prohibited claim.

For thin data, offer useful conservative concepts while labeling evidence limits. Preserve all 11 planned assets in the draft; assets lacking indispensable identity or required product measurements remain `BLOCKED`, with conditional prompts clearly outside the ready-to-copy set. Unknown A+ account settings alone do not block default-canvas planning; mark upload review pending. Record competitor access failures per section, not as a blanket failure when other sections are readable.

## Plan the campaign

Read [reference library](references/reference-library.md) and [set layout dialogue](references/set-layout-dialogue.md) for every full-set project. Start with the bundled `assets/reference-library/catalog.json`, its index and finished reference images; resolve paths relative to this installed skill. Use `scripts/library_catalog.py search --kind series` to find ordered sets, `show SET-ID` to locate images, then `show IMG-ID` or an image-specific recipe branch. A user-supplied external library is optional. This is an internal agent knowledge base, not a user reading/translation workflow. Inspect selected recipes and full images; compare competitor V records with separate LIB candidates. When current competitors remain inaccessible, show two or three compatible historical layouts and use iterative popups to select and refine every affected asset, then adapt the selected image's structure, typography, light and shadows to the actual SKU. Historical references do not fill current review/keyword/competitor coverage or supply product facts. Record confirmed/delegated/proposed choices per asset in `layout-decisions.md`. Do not finalize a pending choice-dependent prompt while awaiting the user; other research and unaffected drafting may continue. A request to choose autonomously or skip discussion overrides the popup workflow.

Read [campaign planning](references/campaign-planning.md) and [product truth and compliance](references/product-truth-compliance.md). Build a product identity lock and a separate visual style lock. Translate cross-competitor visual observations into original layouts: composition zones, product coverage, text hierarchy, camera/view, light direction/softness, contrast, cast/contact shadows, background, palette, depth and props. Distinguish observations from your design proposals, with per-image competitor visual IDs and transfer rationale. Give four scenes different buyer tasks, not four near-identical backgrounds.

For lifestyle and A+ planning, read [scene and occasion expansion](references/scene-expansion.md) and [visual art direction](references/visual-art-direction.md). Proactively derive compatible locations, occasions and seasonal uses from the actual SKU, dimensions, supported placement and competitor evidence; shortlist alternatives before assigning the four scenes. Define architectural anchors, depth, palette proportions, typography and a physically coherent lighting/shadow setup per image. Do not default every asset to a cream console, oversized black headline and faint soft shadow. The [local reference study](references/reference-study.md) supplies historical examples and limits; use the active library workflow above for current candidates. External folders are optional because a portable catalog and selected previews are bundled.

Broaden the shortlist with a bounded public-web inspiration pass when current references are repetitive or incomplete. Search for the actual product category, market, placement, time/season/occasion and styling; inspect useful sources, record what was genuinely read/viewed, and distinguish outside inspiration from competitor V records and our P facts. Select supporting objects for scale, lived-in context or occasion, then keep their salience below the merchandise. The final four scenes are not a fixed set of rooms, times, seasons or props.

Integrate copy into quiet areas of the actual background by default. Avoid routinely separating headlines, captions or facts into colored strips/cards. If a source uses them, preserve its information order while adapting copy to the background; use a separate panel only for a concrete readability need or explicit user choice. Choose scene-appropriate display lettering for atmosphere; facts and units remain clearly readable. For actual typesetting, an agent may load a suitable available local font, recording its file and usage rights; a font name in an image prompt is not a loaded font. Protect the complete product from all type and effects.

Before delivery, resolve conflicting source recipes into one executable direction per image. Match reference names/order to that asset's upload list, budget layout zones and gutters inside the stated margins, and separate measured product dimensions from design assumptions. See the [prompt consistency pass](references/lovart-prompts.md#consistency-pass). When revising prompts after generation, label the existing images as an earlier revision; preserve isolated color edits without silently changing the campaign SKU.

During authorized competitor projects, screen newly collected public references, preserve ASIN/variant/order/source provenance, and deduplicate by content. Explicitly accept or reject every new canonical image after visual review; use approved-only admission so unreviewed/rejected image bytes are not copied into the reusable library. Buyer photos may inform research but are not composition-library defaults; exclude cluttered casual scenes, unreadable graphics and incoherent layouts. Group listing-image variants sharing a layout into reusable recipe families. For A+, assign each template one visually reviewed principal product identity; multiple reference images and image-specific prompt variants may belong to that identity, but a shared layout, parent ASIN or brand never authorizes merging different products. Keep unreviewed identity and multi-product Brand Story/comparison sources outside product template grouping until resolved. Existing restricted archives stay excluded from normal agent retrieval. Before recommending removal of a redundant source directory, verify every image against an independent library copy and check active/rebuild dependencies. This is work in the active project, not an unattended crawl. See [reference library](references/reference-library.md) and `scripts/reference_ingest.py`.

Apply this framework across flowers, potted plants, trees, wreaths and other supported botanical products. Select the relevant product branch first: tabletop container, floor-standing base, hanging support or supported planting/display method; account for live-plant needs where applicable. Wreaths and Christmas palettes in examples are case studies, never global defaults. Derive occasions, palette, scale references, light and shadows anew for each SKU.

Read [scale and composition](references/scale-and-composition.md) for every contextual product image. Build a per-asset scale record from real product dimensions, a named primary reference with stated dimension basis, comparable axes/planes, projected ratio and compatible frame coverage. Keep physical size constant across scenes while allowing camera distance to change. Use the arithmetic helper where available, or show the calculation manually; neither substitutes for visual review. Protect the complete product silhouette from text, blur, gradients and typography effects. Expressive display lettering is optional for short atmosphere copy; facts and dimensions stay clear and readable.

Check current official Amazon requirements for the marketplace, category, and A+ feature set. Record source, date, and what was actually accessible. If verification is unavailable, use conservative planning constraints and label platform review as pending. Templates and third-party repositories are workflow inspiration, not policy authority.

Plan five desktop A+ image assets on the default 1464×600 canvas, then map those assets to the seller's actual Content Manager modules and slots. A multi-image module consumes multiple assets from the same five-image budget; it does not silently increase the count. Unknown entitlement or slot dimensions limits verified upload readiness, not the default-canvas design. If a confirmed slot differs, document the actual slot and adaptation rather than claiming the desktop master is upload-ready. Do not invent account requirements, catalog products, or ASINs. Explain any required extra mobile derivatives before changing the approved scope.

When the user explicitly requests a test and authorizes choosing specifications, select and document a concrete test canvas/module proposal; mark it PROVISIONAL for account upload rather than claiming verified production settings. This does not resolve missing product facts or authorize extra images.

## Write and deliver

Read [deliverables](references/deliverables.md) and [Lovart prompts](references/lovart-prompts.md). Write to a project output folder such as `outputs/<product-slug>/`, keeping source uploads intact:

- `design-strategy.md`: primary Chinese technical/design document, with per-competitor findings, cross-competitor comparisons, access coverage, required evidence sections, 11-image asset map and 11 individual briefs.
- `competitors/<ASIN>.md`: individual research findings and observed visual records, merged into design-strategy rather than replacing its substantive analysis.
- `competitors/<ASIN>.json`: structured checkpoint for coverage, visual evidence, review sampling and recovery attempts; `research-audit.md` summarizes consistency errors and genuine remaining gaps. For multiple marketplaces, use separate project research folders.
- `evidence-ledger.md`: source locators, factual support, observations, sampling limits, contradictions, and risks.
- `discovery.md`: resumable decisions and unanswered questions.
- `layout-decisions.md`: full-set source comparison and the 11 assets' concrete layout choices, user answers/delegation, common style rules and adaptations.
- `prompt-index.md`: readiness, required reference files, A+ slot mapping, and links to every prompt.
- `prompts/<ASSET_ID>.txt`: one self-contained English prompt per actual image; conditional drafts go in `prompts/blocked/` and explain the missing requirement.
- `prompt-manifest.json`: the same 11 IDs, status, file, ordered reference filenames/roles and remaining conditions used to compile the collections.
- `all-prompts.md` and `all-prompts.txt`: copyable prompt collections, one complete prompt per labeled asset; upload references before copying each prompt. Include executable READY/PROVISIONAL prompts with their conditions; list BLOCKED gaps separately without mixing conditional bodies into executable text. Compile from the individual files using `scripts/build_prompt_summary.py`, or preserve the same exact-text invariant manually when Python is unavailable. Do not create a PDF or a controller that asks Lovart to infer all 11 layouts. These are extra file views of the same 11 prompts, not extra image assets.

Every ready prompt must be independently usable: repeat its full identity/style constraints, exact approved copy, reference role mapping, selected LAYOUT BLUEPRINT, and output specification. Translate the chosen source's information structure into explicit zones, window count/shape, typography, dimension lines and reading order while preserving shared campaign rules. Do not rely on another prompt, an internal evidence ID, a local path Lovart cannot access, or unstated conversation history. A+ native text fields belong in the document, not automatically baked into images.

Before delivery, audit the exact 11-image count and IDs, claim-to-evidence links, source-to-layout-decision-to-brief-to-prompt links, contradictory facts, copy/prompt agreement and ready-versus-blocked separation. Verify both collections contain the exact current individual executable prompt texts, ordered references and visible readiness labels, with no PDF. Run the offline research audit where available; fix inconsistent records but never change a blocked status to observed merely to pass. A consistent incomplete record is acceptable for a labeled draft. Check all six secondary images and five A+ images have individual briefs and prompts. State what was researched, what is provisional, and what needs user action. Do not claim image fidelity, reference-layout equivalence, rendered text correctness, platform acceptance, or conversion uplift from prompt review alone.

Also audit the scene-search coverage and shortlist, rejection reasons, four-scene task diversity, residential localization, physical/projected scale consistency, product-versus-prop salience, five-image A+ narrative, complete product protection from text effects, palette roles and light-to-shadow geometry. Check that default/overridden canvas specifications agree across briefs, index and prompts. Strong sunlight, window shadows, backlight and warm/cool evening contrast are valid design tools when motivated by the scene; soft low-contrast lighting is a choice, not the universal default.
